# Gate 1 — Privacy and Retention Matrix

Per Section 26's required categories, plus ADR-012 (retention enforced by
table-level `data_class` tags). Retention durations marked "pending DL-011"
are mechanism-ready but need your/legal's actual number before Gate 6.

| Data category | Purpose | Visibility | Encryption | Retention | Deletion behavior | Legal basis / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search location (ad hoc "near me" query) | Power a single discovery query | Server-side only, never stored against identity beyond the request | In transit (TLS); not persisted beyond request-scoped cache | Ephemeral — not written to durable storage unless the user saves the search | N/A (nothing to delete) | Consent: implicit in using location-based search, revocable by denying the OS permission |
| Active tracking (exploration session, fog-of-war) | Opt-in exploration game feature (Section 20) | Private to the user; regional progress only shown as town/county-level aggregates | Encrypted at rest | Session-scoped; historical fog tiles retained until user deletes fog history (QB-121) | User-initiated full delete via `DELETE /me/exploration-history`; deleting fog history must not delete unrelated achievements (QB-121, acceptance scenario 15) — these live in separate tables | Consent: explicit opt-in per session; background tracking requires a separate, additional permission (Section 20, 29) |
| Fog history (`MapTileDiscovery`, `RegionProgress`) | Long-term exploration progress display | Private, with only region-level names shown publicly if the user opts into sharing | Encrypted at rest | Until user deletes or account closes | Selective delete (fog only) without touching `Badge`/`Achievement` rows, per acceptance scenario 15 | Same consent basis as active tracking |
| Evidence (`Evidence` — photos/video/GPS/answers) | Prove quest completion, support disputes | Visibility per quest's evidence policy; never public by default | Encrypted at rest; EXIF stripped server-side before storage (Section 26) | Pending DL-011 — recommended: retain through the dispute/appeal window (Section 24) plus a fixed buffer, then auto-delete | Scheduled job purges past-retention evidence; user can also request early deletion subject to open-dispute holds | Legal basis: contract performance (completion verification) + legitimate interest (dispute resolution) |
| Party location (`TemporaryLocationShare`) | Coordinate an active in-person party/quest | Visible only to named party members, only while active | Encrypted at rest and in transit | Hard-expires with the active quest window (Section 26) — no "always on" party location ever | Automatic purge on expiry, not just a UI hide | Consent: explicit per-party opt-in; matches Section 26's "temporary party/family location sharing exists only during an active quest, with clear participants and expiration" |
| Home inventory (`Inventory`, `InventoryItem`) | Power Hearth/pantry-based generation | Private by default (QB-081) | Encrypted at rest | Until user edits/deletes; exportable on demand | User-initiated edit/export/delete; image-extraction proposals (`InventoryExtraction`) are deleted immediately if not confirmed | Consent: explicit opt-in to maintain inventory; photo extraction requires confirmation before persisting |
| AI conversations (`AiConversationLog`, `GenerationJob` I/O) | Debugging, quality evaluation, abuse investigation | Internal only; never shown to other users | Encrypted at rest; access-logged | Pending DL-011-adjacent decision — recommended: shorter fixed window (e.g., tied to the AI eval/quality-review cycle) with redaction of precise location before long-term retention | Scheduled redaction/purge job; user erase request removes their identifiable conversation content subject to abuse-investigation holds | Section 14's "input/output logging with privacy controls and retention rules" — needs the same legal-basis review as evidence |
| Moderation records (`Report`, `ModerationCase`, `SafetyIncident`, `AuditEvent`) | Trust & safety operations, appeals, legal defense | Internal, moderator/admin only | Encrypted at rest | Longer retention than user-content categories — moderation/audit history is explicitly *not* deleted on user account deletion if "legally or operationally required for past completions, disputes, safety, or audit history" (Section 22) | Retained on legal/audit hold even if the underlying account is deleted; only purged per a separate, longer compliance-driven schedule | Legal basis: legitimate interest (platform safety), legal obligation (dispute/audit defense) |
| Child data (`ChildProfile`, `GuardianRelationship`, all child-linked attempts/evidence) | Enable supervised participation for minors | Strictly private; no public profile, no public leaderboard/photo-sharing by default (QB-181) | Encrypted at rest, stricter access logging than adult data | Shortest reasonable retention consistent with the guardian relationship; deleted promptly on guardian request except where a moderation hold applies | Guardian-initiated export/erase; independent of the child's own action since children don't hold independent credentials | Legal basis: parental consent (guardian account holder); heightened compliance posture pending DL-002's age-threshold decision |
| Account data (`User`, `Profile`, `Consent`, `AgeAttestation`) | Core identity and authentication | Public fields per `Profile.public_fields`; rest private | Password hashed (never reversible); PII encrypted at rest | Until account deletion, subject to the moderation-record carve-out above | Full export (QB-183) and full erase, with moderation/audit records surviving per the carve-out above | Legal basis: contract (providing the service) |
| Ratings/reviews (`Rating`, `Review`) | Community quality signal | Public once aggregate threshold met (Section 23); individual rating never exposes location | Encrypted at rest | Retained as long as the reviewed `QuestVersion` exists, for the historical-attempt-version reason in ADR-007 | Anonymized rather than hard-deleted on user erase where deletion would corrupt aggregate quality signals others rely on — flag as a legal-review item alongside DL-011 | Legal basis: legitimate interest (community trust); balanced against user erase rights |

## Cross-cutting rules

- **No category above uses "delete the account row and hope cascades work
  correctly" as the deletion mechanism.** ADR-012's `data_class` tagging
  drives one central export/erase job that knows exactly which tables to
  touch, in what order, and which rows are exempt under a legal/moderation
  hold — this is a Gate 2 implementation requirement, not just documentation.
- **EXIF stripping happens server-side at upload time**, not client-side only
  (a client-side-only strip is trivially bypassed by a modified client or
  direct API call) — applies to evidence photos/videos and any profile/quest
  imagery.
- **Guest users generate no durable personal-data footprint** beyond the
  ephemeral session/device-local preferences described in Gate 0's
  resolution of Contradiction C-5 — there's no "guest data" row to retain or
  delete in the first place.
